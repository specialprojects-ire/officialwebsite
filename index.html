About Trinethra

Three lenses on every chart, every claim, every line of code.

Trinethra means “three eyes” in Sanskrit. The name sums up how we work: every engagement is reviewed for clinical accuracy, financial performance and regulatory compliance at the same time.

Trinethra Global Healthcare Technology Solutions Pvt Ltd brings together certified coding and revenue cycle professionals, clinical documentation specialists and software engineers. Our teams in India work alongside healthcare organizations worldwide, with particular depth in the US healthcare system.

Clinical accuracy

Codes that reflect the true severity of care, assigned and checked against current ICD-10, CPT and payer guidelines.

Financial clarity

Fewer denials, shorter AR cycles and transparent reporting, so finance leaders see exactly where revenue is gained or lost.

Compliance by design

HIPAA-compliant processes, strict access controls and documented audit trails built into every workflow.

To be the most trusted global partner for healthcare revenue integrity and technology, known for accuracy, transparency and care.

Technology

AI that speeds experts up. Experts who keep the AI honest.

Our human-in-the-loop pipeline turns unstructured clinical documentation into accurate, auditable codes and claims. Every automated suggestion is traceable to the source text and confirmed by a credentialed professional.

  1. 01

    Secure ingestion

    Encrypted intake from EHRs, document systems and SFTP, with no PHI stored on local workstations.

  2. 02

    Clinical NLP

    Language models extract diagnoses, procedures and evidence, then suggest codes with confidence scores.

  3. 03

    Expert validation

    Certified coders review each suggestion against guidelines and payer edits, followed by a second-level QA sample.

  4. 04

    Delivery & insight

    Codes and claims flow back into your billing system, with dashboards for productivity, accuracy and denials.
